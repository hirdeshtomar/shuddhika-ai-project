Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Shuddhika Lead Management ("the App") is an internal business tool operated by Shuddhika Pure Mustard Oil for managing sales leads and customer outreach.

Information We Collect

The App is used by authorized staff only and requires a login. We store: staff account details (name, email), business lead information (business names, phone numbers, addresses collected from public directories or entered manually), and records of outreach messages sent and received through the WhatsApp Business API.

How Information Is Used

Data is used solely to manage sales outreach for Shuddhika Pure Mustard Oil: contacting potential business buyers, tracking conversations, and measuring campaign results. We do not sell, rent, or share data with third parties for advertising.

Third-Party Services

The App uses the WhatsApp Business Cloud API (Meta) to send and receive messages, and Google Places API to discover publicly listed businesses. Use of those services is governed by their respective privacy policies.

Opt-Out

Any business contacted through the App can reply STOP (or similar) at any time and will not be contacted again.

Data Security

Data is stored in access-controlled databases. Only authenticated staff can access the App.
