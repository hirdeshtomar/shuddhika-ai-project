## Full description (4000 chars max)
Shuddhika Lead Management is the internal sales tool for Shuddhika Pure Mustard Oil staff.

Find and manage business leads — kirana stores, oil wholesalers, restaurants, sweet shops and pickle manufacturers — and reach them through approved WhatsApp Business templates.

Features:
• Lead list sorted by relevance, with one-tap calling
• WhatsApp conversations with customers and prospects, in one inbox
• Campaign management with safe, rate-limited sending
• Instant notifications for new leads and incoming messages
• Lead discovery from business directories by city and category

This app is intended for authorized Shuddhika staff. A staff login is required.
